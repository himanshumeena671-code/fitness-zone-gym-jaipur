Why Fitness Zone

Training that is planned, not guessed

Everything below comes from what members and the gym itself say about the place — no marketing promises, just how it actually runs.

22 years of experience

Coaching members continuously since 17 September 1998 — more than two decades of hands-on floor experience at the same gym.

Scientific & structured training

Workouts are planned rather than improvised. Members describe the coaching approach as scientific and structured, with every session following a clear method.

Individual attention

Every member is given individual attention on the floor, so each workout stays effective and — importantly — safe for your body and your level.

Pain-relief exercises

A dedicated pain-relief exercise option is offered for people training around aches and stiffness. Call to check whether it suits your condition.
